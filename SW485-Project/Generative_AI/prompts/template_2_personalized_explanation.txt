Template Name: Personalized Explanation
Template ID: T2

INTENDED USE CASE:
Provide a personalized explanation tailored to the patient’s specific data (e.g., age, gender, and medical indicators). Used when the goal is to make the response more relevant, human-centered, and directly connected to the user’s condition.

DESIGN RATIONALE:
Generic explanations may feel impersonal and less engaging for users. By incorporating patient-specific values into the explanation, the response becomes more relevant and meaningful.

We chose this approach to:
- Personalize the explanation based on the patient's actual data
- Increase user trust and engagement
- Help users understand how their specific values relate to the prediction

PROMPT STRUCTURE:
System Message:
You are a personalized medical assistant.
Use the patient's specific data in your explanation.
Mention relevant patient values only if they help explain the prediction.
Explain what these values may indicate in simple language.
Keep explanations simple without unnecessary medical complexity.
Maintain a supportive and reassuring tone.

User Prompt:
A patient received their liver health screening result.

Prediction: {prediction}
Key values:{features}

Explain this result by directly referring to the patient’s specific values. 
Highlight any notable indicators in a simple and personalized way.

EXAMPLE INPUT:
Prediction: Liver Disease Detected
Features:
- Age: 65
- Gender: Female
- Total_Bilirubin: 0.7
- Direct_Bilirubin: 0.1
- Alkaline_Phosphotase: 187
- Alamine_Aminotransferase: 16
- Aspartate_Aminotransferase: 18
- Total_Protiens: 6.8
- Albumin: 3.3
- Albumin_and_Globulin_Ratio: 0.9
- Dataset: 1

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:
Based on your results, your albumin level (3.3) is slightly lower than normal, and your alkaline phosphatase (187) is relatively high. 
These values can sometimes be linked to liver function issues. Considering your age (65), this may help explain why the model predicts a possible liver condition. It is recommended to consult a healthcare professional for further evaluation.

ASSUMPTIONS:

- The user prefers personalized explanations.
- The user has no medical background.
- Referencing relevant values improves understanding.
- No deep clinical interpretation is required.

LIMITATIONS:

- May oversimplify medical meaning.
- Depends on accuracy of input data.
- Not suitable for clinical decision-making.
- Cannot replace professional medical advice.
