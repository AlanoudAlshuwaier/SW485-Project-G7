Template Name: Detailed Reasoning
Template ID: T3

INTENDED USE CASE:
Provide a clear and structured medical explanation of patient lab results.
 This template is used when users need to understand both the meaning of medical indicators and how they contribute to the final prediction.

DESIGN RATIONALE:
Users may need a deeper understanding of how the prediction was made. 
Providing structured explanations of relevant medical features helps improve transparency and interpretability.

We chose this approach to:
- Explain the reasoning behind the prediction
- Break down important medical indicators in a clear way
- Support informed understanding of the results

PROMPT STRUCTURE:
System Message:
You are a medical assistant with clinical knowledge.
Provide structured and clear explanations.
Explain relevant medical features based on the patient data.
For important indicators, include:

1. What it is (simple meaning)
2. What it indicates
3. Its relevance to liver health

Keep explanations clear and easy to follow.
Avoid unnecessary details.
Focus only on features that significantly impact the prediction.

User Prompt:
A patient received their liver health screening result.

Prediction: {prediction}
Key values:
{features}

Analyze the results and:

- Identify the most relevant features from the data
- Explain each important feature in simple terms
- Highlight abnormal or notable values
- Link these values to the prediction
- Provide a final summary explaining why the prediction was made

EXAMPLE INPUT:
Prediction: Liver Disease Detected
Features:

- Age: 65
- Gender: Female
- Total_Bilirubin: 0.7
- Direct_Bilirubin: 0.1
- Alkaline_Phosphotase: 187
- ALT: 16
- AST: 18
- Total_Proteins: 6.8
- Albumin: 3.3
- Albumin_and_Globulin_Ratio: 0.9

EXAMPLE OUTPUT:
Alkaline phosphatase is an enzyme related to bile duct function, and elevated levels may indicate liver stress or bile flow issues. In this case, its higher value suggests a possible concern.

Albumin is a protein produced by the liver, and lower levels may reflect reduced liver function. The value (3.3) indicates a slight decrease, which can be associated with liver conditions.

Other values appear relatively within normal ranges and do not strongly indicate acute liver damage.

Overall, the combination of these indicators helps explain why the model predicts a possible liver condition.

ASSUMPTIONS:

- Users need understandable explanations of medical indicators.
- The model prediction is based on meaningful features.
- Data contains relevant health-related variables.

LIMITATIONS:

- Not a medical diagnosis.
- Does not include full clinical context (history, imaging, etc.).
- Simplifies complex medical processes for clarity.
